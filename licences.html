Licences

Four licences, 32 tests.

Every licence starts with timed tests on real circuits. Earn the medals, race the cups and the next licence opens.

  1. Stage 1National B8 tests
  2. Stage 2National A8 tests
  3. Stage 3International8 tests
  4. Stage 4Pro8 tests

Bronze, silver and gold.

Every test has three target times. Each medal pays prize money, and gold in every test of a licence earns a bonus car for your garage.

  • Attempts only count from a standing start at the lights.
  • Off track or without reaching the finish, a run does not count.
  • Your best run of a session stays on the board.
