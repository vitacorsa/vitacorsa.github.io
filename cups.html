How a race weekend works.

  1. Session 1

    Practice

    Optional, and you can end it at any time.

  2. Session 2

    Qualifying

    About three laps, long enough for the whole field to set a time. Your lap sets the grid, and pole pays a bonus.

  3. Session 3

    Race

    The game runs the race with its physics, AI and weather. The field follows your pace within the AI level you chose.

  4. Session 4

    Result

    Points, prize money and reputation, your rivalry and the team standings, then on to the next round.

Race formats

About every third race comes with a twist, fixed when you enter and shown in the cup calendar.

  • Reversed grid
  • Success ballast
  • Point for fastest lap
  • Rain race
  • Dusk race
