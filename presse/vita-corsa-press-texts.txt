LONG, ABOUT 250 WORDS

Vita Corsa is a free, unofficial career mod for Assetto Corsa EVO. It opens from its own tile in the game's main menu and turns the game's cars and circuits into a career: from National B through National A and International to the Pro licence.

Every licence starts with timed tests on real circuits. 32 tests pay prize money for bronze, silver and gold, and gold in every test of a licence earns a bonus car. The next licence asks for medals, clean races and cup results.

33 cups with 143 races run as real weekends: optional practice, a short qualifying that sets the grid, and a field that follows the player's pace. Race formats such as a reversed grid, success ballast, rain and dusk races keep a season varied, and a title calculator shows what is needed before the final.

A regular cast of 112 fictional drivers in 30 teams is the same in every career. Drivers have traits, form and rivalries; rivals remember close fights and move up the ladder with the player.

A career is built to last: milestones keep its special moments, long-term goals give it a direction, and a retired career goes into a hall of fame. Players can add pressure to a cup or start in iron mode, where every race counts. A Basic career without teams, sponsors and press is there for players who just want to race.

Dealers sell the 71 cars of the game at researched market prices in 7 currencies. A garage with workshop, season sponsors, 36 cup sponsor brands, invitations and works contracts complete the economy, which stays separate from the game's own. Installed car mods can join 15 cups if the player switches them on.

Vita Corsa is free, runs on Windows with the Steam version of Assetto Corsa EVO and is available in English and German. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Kunos Simulazioni or 505 Games.
