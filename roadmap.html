Next version · 0.4.0

9 of 10 done

A career that lasts: milestones, long-term goals and a hall of fame, challenge levels with an iron mode, and drivers with a story.

Race weekend & AIDone · ships with 0.4.0

Drivers with a story

The regular drivers earn traits from what happens in your races, like a rain specialist after strong races in the wet or a slump after a run of weak cups. Rivalries grow from a duel into a feud and into the one that defines your career, and the press remembers what you said before. On track they still race with the game's own AI.

VarietyDone · ships with 0.4.0

Weekly challenge

Every week one fixed challenge, the same for everyone: same circuit, same car, same conditions. You race the times of 15 drivers from the paddock, as often as you like. A medal brings a small prize and reputation, a skipped week only pauses your streak, and every fourth week with a medal pays double. It runs on your own PC, without online leaderboards.

VarietyDone · ships with 0.4.0

A lighter career

A Basic career for players who just want to race: licences, cups, prize money, dealers and your garage. Teams, sponsors, press, the driver market and the gala are switched off. You pick it when you start a career and can switch between Basic and Full in the settings whenever no cup is running. Together with the short season option it makes for quick evenings.

Community wish
PresentationDone · ships with 0.4.0

Milestones

Your driver file keeps the special moments of your career: your first pole, your first win in the rain, your hundredth start, a win from tenth on the grid. Careers you have already started get their milestones too.

PresentationDone · ships with 0.4.0

Career goals and a legacy

Pick three long-term goals, such as a title in every licence or making your team the best in the paddock, and see them judged in your yearbook. After the Pro title new goals keep you going. When you retire, a hall of fame looks back on your whole career, and a new career can start with one car from your old garage.

PresentationDone · ships with 0.4.0

Small things that help

Bookmark up to five cars at the dealer and see what you are still short of. Open the title fight chart from the running cup, with your gap after every round. Get a report card with a grade when you move up a licence. And switch to a short season with about half the rounds per cup.

Economy & teamDone · ships with 0.4.0

Challenge levels and iron mode

Before a cup you can make it harder on purpose: stronger opponents, full damage, a start from the back, more rain or a double entry fee. The harder you choose, the more prize money and reputation you earn, and a trophy won that way carries a plaque. For the bravest there is an iron mode, chosen when you start a career: every race you start counts, repairs cost the full amount and running out of money ends the career.

Economy & teamDone · ships with 0.4.0

Promises to team and sponsor

When a team or a season sponsor signs you, choose what you promise: a safe target, a normal one or an ambitious one with a bigger bonus. Keep your word and they trust you more, break it and the relationship cools step by step. Teams that think well of you offer contracts over two or three cups with a season bonus, and you can leave a team contract early for a fee.

FixesDone · ships with 0.4.0

Safer first start

If the game starts without Vita Corsa because the Steam launch option is missing or broken, you are told what to fix. A new start menu entry collects the log files for a bug report, with your user name removed.

More contentPlanned

Italian interface

Vita Corsa in Italian as a third language next to English and German. More languages follow if players ask for them.

Not possible or not planned

7

What the game does not allow today, and what we leave out on purpose.

Race weekend & AINot possible

Penalties and track limits

In offline races the game does not hand out penalties for cutting the track, it only cancels the lap time. Without penalties from the game there is nothing fair to score, so this stays out.

Race weekend & AINot possible

AI racecraft and overtaking

How AI cars fight for position is decided inside the game, and mods cannot change it. We will look again when the game's new AI arrives.

Community wish
PresentationNot possible

3D podium ceremony

The game has no podium scene to use. Vita Corsa shows its own trophy and title scenes instead.

PresentationNot possible

Official circuit logos

The game has no circuit logos to show, and we do not copy them. Vita Corsa uses its own circuit signs instead.
