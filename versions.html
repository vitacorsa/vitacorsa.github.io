Versions

Every version, every change.

All releases of Vita Corsa with their changes, downloads and SHA-256 checksums. Compare a checksum before you run the setup.

  1. 0.4.1

    11 November 2026 Current Download ZIP, 8.1 MB (version 0.4.1)
    • Setup next to other mod tools: Setup no longer stops with "No valid file table" when another tool has added cars or tracks to the game's own data file. Vita Corsa also builds its track list on what other mods changed there, so their extra tracks stay in the game.
    • Setup finds the right game folder: with several Steam libraries Setup could pick an old copy of the game. It now prefers the installation Steam actually runs.
    • Dusk races at the right time: at Spa a dusk race started in the dark, at Oulton Park too late and at Mount Panorama too early, because the game keeps its own clock and position for these circuits. Start times now follow the game's sun at every circuit.
    • New version notice: when a new version is out, the game's main menu tells you once per game start, the Vita Corsa tile shows it, and the settings list it next to your own version. For this the launcher looks up a version number on vitacorsa.github.io when the game starts, at most once a day. It downloads nothing and sends nothing about you. Switch it off under Settings › New version notice.
    • Latest changes: the list of changes is now in the game, under Menu › Latest changes or from the version line in the settings. It shows this version and the two before it and, when a new version is out, what it brings.

  2. 0.4.0

    11 November 2026 Download ZIP, 7.5 MB (version 0.4.0)
    • Safer first start: if the game runs without the Vita Corsa launcher (Steam launch option missing or broken), Vita Corsa tells you in the game how to fix it.
    • New start menu entry "Collect logs": puts all logs for a bug report in a ZIP on your desktop, with your user name removed.
    • Milestones: 48 special moments of your career in eight groups, from your first win to a win from the back of the grid, a win in the rain, ten track records or a title without a defeat. Each one keeps its day, track and car. Open them in the driver file; new ones show in the race result, the chronicle and the paddock news. Careers you have already started get the milestones their races allow when you load them.
    • Career goals: after your first finished cup, pick three of six long-term goals, such as titles in all four classes, ten track records or making your own team number one. You may swap one goal each season. When all three are reached, or after your first Pro title, new goals follow. The yearbook shows which goals you reached in each season.
    • Retirement: end a career in the settings. Its record goes into a hall of fame that every save slot can open, and a new career can start in the same slot with one car from the old garage, with its upgrades and history.
    • Pressure: when you enter a cup you can make it harder on purpose. Pick any of five handicaps in the sponsor window: a field one AI level stronger, full damage for this cup, a start from the back without qualifying, more rain or a double entry fee. Every point adds 5 % prize money and reputation for that cup, and a trophy won under pressure carries a plaque in your trophy cabinet.
    • Iron mode, chosen when you start a career and fixed after that: every race you start counts, so leaving a session without a result is a retirement with last place and no prize money. Damage is fixed at 100 % and repairs cost the full amount. If your balance drops below zero you must settle it before the next start (sell a car, take an advance); if nothing covers the debt, the career ends in the hall of fame. In return every race pays 10 % more prize money and reputation.
    • Drivers with a story: the regular drivers earn up to two traits from what happens in your races, shown on the driver card with the track where they earned them. Rain specialist (three podiums in the wet, one of them a win), serial winner, champion, home hero and comeback make a driver a little stronger in the matching races; a slump or finale nerves make them weaker until a strong cup or a title turns it around.
    • Rivalries grow: a duel becomes a feud after eight close races in which both of you have beaten the other, or sooner once you have called a driver out in the press. A feud rival turns into a hothead, and every close duel you win against them brings reputation. One rival per career becomes the rival of your career. The stage shows in the driver file, the race briefing, the yearbook and the hall of fame, with its own headlines.
    • The press remembers: now and then a question picks up what you said before. Deliver after a bold claim and you gain more; fail and stay defiant, or blame race control after promising to clean up, and it costs reputation. Careers you have already started get traits and rivalries from the races still in their history.
    • Promises: when a team signs you, you choose what you promise. Safe, normal or ambitious set how high the goal is, for example top 5, top 3 or the title, and the bonus follows (60, 100 or 160 %). Keep your word and the team likes you more; break it and the relationship cools, more so the more you promised.
    • Season contracts: teams that think well of you, and the works teams, now also offer two or three cups in a row. Your promise counts for each cup, and if you keep them all the team adds a season bonus. Cups that belong to the contract are reserved until it is their turn.
    • Leave a team contract: the cup page has a "Leave team" button. It costs a fee (half the bonuses still open, at least the entry fee) and the team will remember it. If the cup is already running it is abandoned as well.
    • Main sponsor: after picking the brand you choose your promise for every cup (top 5, top 3 or the title) with the cup bonus to match. Warnings are gone: a kept promise lifts the sponsor's mood, a broken one lowers it, and only at the lowest mood does the next broken promise end the contract. Running contracts keep their terms; a warning you already had counts as a lowered mood.
    • Bookmarks: "Bookmark" at the dealer now keeps up to five cars. A new "Bookmarks" entry in the filter row lists them with their price and what you are still short of. The first one stays your savings goal on Home.
    • Title fight from the running cup: the cup page has a "Title fight" link below the standings. The chart now also shows your gap to your rival after every round.
    • Report card: when you move up a licence you get a report card for the class you just finished: races, wins, podiums, titles, test medals, clean races and prize money, with a grade (passed, with merit, with distinction) from five criteria. It stays in the licence book. Careers that have already moved up get theirs when you load them.
    • Short season: new setting "Season length". With "Short", cups run about half of their rounds (3 or 4 become 2, 5 or 6 become 3) and keep the first round and the final. Entry fee, cup bonus and bonuses per cup drop in the same ratio, prize money and points per round stay. You can switch while no cup is running; a finished cup keeps its length.
    • Cup calendar: long track names appear in their short paddock form (Sebring, Watkins Glen, Spa, COTA) instead of being cut off.
    • Fixed: the small link buttons, for example on Home, were unreadable while selected with a controller or the keyboard.
    • A field at your pace: opponents now drive the eligible cars that are closest to your own car in pace, measured in the game for every car. In a slow car you race the slow cars of the cup, in a fast one the fast ones. Before, a cup always mixed all its cars, for example 83 hp and 300 hp in the first cup. You can still enter any eligible car. Cups you have already entered keep their field.
    • Opponents in different liveries: the regular drivers no longer all drive the first factory colour. Each driver gets a livery of their own for a model. Models that come with a single livery in the game look the same.
    • Racing page: pick the licence level at the top, like on the Licences page. Rounds, starters, entry fee and prize money now stand above the calendar, and a cup lists all its eligible cars instead of the first four.
    • Mail in the Paddock: press questions, invitations, team offers and contracts now arrive on the Paddock page. The Racing page shows races only (in a Basic career the mail stays on Racing).
    • Time trials and the weekly challenge moved to the Licences page, below the tests.
    • Dealer: brands with more than four models have arrows left and right of the car pictures, and the pictures stay in the same place for every brand.
    • Fixed: every car in every save keeps its own paint. Before, all cars of one model shared the paint chosen last, across all saves. The car you use first after the update keeps the old paint; other cars of that model start in their factory colour.
    • Fixed: opening and closing the pause menu during a licence test or time trial cancelled the attempt (no time shown, finish not counted). The attempt now continues.
    • Fixed: the dealer's cup filter showed a cup from the optional extra content although extra content was switched off.

  3. 0.3.5

    9 November 2026 26 downloads on GitHub Download ZIP, 7.4 MB (version 0.3.5)
    • Paint on track: the livery you choose in the game's paint shop (Garage, "Paint & looks") is now the one your car races in. Before, the car went on track in the colour it was bought in unless it had upgrades fitted.
    • New Paddock tab next to Racing: teams of your licence, your own team, paddock news and the track book. Racing shows races and invitations again.
    • Leave a running cup: the cup page has an "Abandon cup" button. The entry fee is lost and your reputation drops by 10, the races you drove stay in your history, and you can enter the cup again later.
    • One currency: all prices are shown in your own currency, also in the car lists of the cups. The dealer adds the local price as a small note.
    • The dealer opened from the main menu always lists every car. Only the way in from a cup shows just the cars allowed there.
    • Mouse wheel: long lists scroll smoothly under the pointer instead of jumping, and show a scroll bar you can drag or click.
    • Invitations show how long they are valid, in the list and in the letter.
    • The menus use the same red as the game's own menus.
    • Awards evening: at maximum reputation it no longer says you won nothing when you took an award.

  4. 0.3.0

    8 November 2026 23 downloads on GitHub Download ZIP, 7.4 MB (version 0.3.0)
    • Paddock: 30 fictional teams across the four licences, each with its own character and favourite make. Teams offer you a seat between cups: they provide the car, pay the entry, set a team goal and remember how it went. A new Paddock page shows every team and its drivers.
    • Driver market: every four cups drivers change teams, the best young talents step up a licence, veterans retire and newcomers arrive. A letter tells you who moved, and results in your races count.
    • Paddock news: two or three headlines after every race. After a big moment a reporter asks you a question, and each of the three answers shows its effect on your reputation, your sponsor's mood or your rival first.
    • Seasons: every four cups end with an awards evening with five awards and three nominees each. A yearbook in the driver file keeps every season.
    • Your own team from the International licence: name, race number, a team-mate from the paddock and a team budget of its own.
    • An advance on prize money for you or your team, paid back automatically with half of every prize.
    • New career start: choose a background (classic, talent, gentleman driver or factory junior in a youth team).
    • Track book: your best lap on every circuit in every car, wet laps kept apart, and circuit records against the regular drivers in your races.
    • Time trials: twelve challenges in a provided car on corner sequences and full laps, three per licence, with four new circuits (Kyalami, Road Atlanta, Watkins Glen, Sebring).
    • Sprint and feature race as a new round format: a short sprint for the top 8, then the full race in the order the sprint finished.
    • Text size setting: normal, large (110 %) or extra large (120 %).
    • Game updates: Vita Corsa checks itself at start-up, tells you once what it found and pauses only the part that no longer works. The game version and the result are shown in the settings.
    • Licence tests: fairer medal times. Silver matches the AI from a standing start, gold asks for a little more and bronze leaves room. Times you have already driven are rated again, and a better medal pays the difference.
    • Licence tests: "Try again" could leave the start lights stuck on one red light after a long session. It now restarts the session when needed, and the result page always preselects "Try again".
    • Licence tests: a run-up in reverse before the start gate no longer counts. Rolling back more than 2 m after the car is ready ends the attempt like a false start.
    • Settings now show the Vita Corsa version next to the game version, so you can see at a glance that Vita Corsa is running.
    • Faster menus: the showroom loads the car you pick straight away instead of pausing first, the driver file opens without a hitch even after many seasons, and the Vita Corsa tile appears as soon as the main menu does.
    • Start page: the reputation line in the career box no longer gets cut off at some reputation levels.

  5. 0.2.0

    3 November 2026 82 downloads on GitHub Download ZIP, 7.3 MB (version 0.2.0)
    • Career: qualifying that counts, a field that follows your pace, rivals with a memory, a real season finale with match point, a regular cast of 112 fictional drivers, 12 teams with team standings and team-mates, race formats (reversed grid, success ballast, evening and dusk races), a season sponsor and 36 sponsor brands, 20 invitations, three new cups, trophies and title scenes, circuit signs and a career start screen.
    • Interface: lap times on the result page, grid by pace in the race preparation, manufacturer logos shown from the game, clearer reasons on team cars, calm transitions (in VR everything only fades).
    • If a game update no longer fits Vita Corsa, Windows now tells you so with a short notification, and the game starts without Vita Corsa as before. If only the licence tests no longer fit, the rest of the career keeps running and only the licence tests are switched off until an update.
    • While Vita Corsa adapts to a new game version (more than 2 seconds), a notification says so. A failed adaptation is not repeated at every start.
    • Setup: clearer error messages with the next step and the help address (https://vitacorsa.github.io), a warning before an older setup replaces a newer version, the moved game folder is detected on update.
    • Uninstall: works even if an antivirus removed the starter; the remaining files are listed with their path. The "Remove Steam link" window now fits its text.
    • New sections in README.txt: "Antivirus and SmartScreen" and "The game does not start any more".
    • Runs alongside ACE UI App Loader: Vita Corsa now loads before other UI mods, and the setup no longer stops with code 13.
    • Licence tests: an attempt only counts from a standing start at the lights; every attempt of a session is kept for your best time.
    • Saves: if the game cannot deliver its storage, Vita Corsa shows "Try again" instead of empty slots and never overwrites a career it could not read.

  6. 0.1.0

    30 October 2026 54 downloads on GitHub Download ZIP, 7.2 MB (version 0.1.0)
    • First public version as a Windows setup: per-user installation without administrator rights, listed under "Installed apps".
    • The mod package is built and verified from your own game installation during setup; the starter rebuilds it by itself after game updates.
    • Guided Steam setup with a copy button; guided removal when uninstalling. Steam files are never changed automatically.
    • Way out: hold Shift when starting through Steam = this one time without Vita Corsa; the next normal start brings Vita Corsa back.
    • Updates by running the new setup: careers, extra content and other mods are kept; outdated own files are removed selectively.
    • Content: career with licence path (B, A, International, Pro) and licence tests, cups, dealers, garage, sponsors, invitations, free career, extra content (ZUSATZINHALTE.md, German) and installed mod cars at the dealer, in matching cups and as AI opponents (both optional, off by default).
    • "Schnitt" interface: car stage in the showroom (Home, Garage, Dealer, Workshop, invitations) with a photo stage as fallback; boards for Racing, cups, race preparation, licences, career path, driver record and settings; dedicated moments for titles, race results, licence tests and new licences; one icon family (Tabler Icons, MIT). Controller, keyboard, mouse.
    • Own menu tile with the Vita Corsa signet; brands appear by name, without manufacturer logos; own terms (VITA-CORSA-TERMS.txt).
