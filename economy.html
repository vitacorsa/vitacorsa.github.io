Dealers sell the 71 cars of AC EVO from 26 makers at researched market prices. Filter by cup, budget and powertrain, and see each price in the local currency and in your home currency.

  • Bookmark up to five cars and see their price and what you are still short of.

Your garage and workshop.

Your cars stand on the showroom stage. The workshop shows specs and mechanical parts, repairs damage after a race and sells cars you no longer need. A shortcut opens the game's own paint and livery editor, and every car keeps its own paint.

Career money stays in the career.

Career money and garage belong to Vita Corsa and are separate from the game's own economy. Results earn prize money and reputation; the AI level you choose sets how much a result pays.

Sponsors with their own goals.

A season sponsor per licence backs you through its cups. After picking the brand you choose your promise for every cup: top 5, top 3 or the title, with the cup bonus to match. Small local brands back you at national level, big names only further up.

  • A kept promise lifts the sponsor's mood, a broken one lowers it.
  • Only at the lowest mood does the next broken promise end the contract.

Team seats and works contracts.

Reputation opens doors: team seats with a team-mate in the same car, and works contracts with a car from the team. Workshop, paint and sale of a team car stay with the team.

  • When a team signs you, you choose what you promise: safe, normal or ambitious. The bonus follows.
  • Keep your word and the team likes you more. Break it and the relationship cools, more so the more you promised.
  • Teams that think well of you, and the works teams, also offer two or three cups in a row with a season bonus.
  • You can leave a team contract for a fee, and the team will remember it.
