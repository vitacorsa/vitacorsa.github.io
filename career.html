Career

A career that lasts.

Milestones, long-term goals and a hall of fame give you reasons for the tenth season. Make it harder with pressure or iron mode, or lighter with a Basic career and short seasons.

Milestones worth keeping.

48 special moments of your career in eight groups, from your first win to a win from the back of the grid, a win in the rain, ten track records or a title without a defeat. Each one keeps its day, track and car.

  • Open them in your driver file.
  • New ones show in the race result, the chronicle and the paddock news.
  • Careers you have already started get the milestones their races allow.

Three goals for the long run.

After your first finished cup you pick three of six long-term goals, such as titles in all four classes, ten track records or making your own team number one. The yearbook shows which goals you reached in each season.

  • You may swap one goal each season.
  • When all three are reached, or after your first Pro title, new goals follow.

Retire into the hall of fame.

You can end a career in the settings. Its record goes into a hall of fame that every save slot can open. A new career can start in the same slot with one car from the old garage, with its upgrades and history.

Pressure, when you want more.

When you enter a cup you can make it harder on purpose. Each handicap carries points, and every point adds 5% prize money and reputation for that cup. A trophy won under pressure carries a plaque in your trophy cabinet.

  • A field one AI level stronger.
  • Full damage for this cup.
  • A start from the back without qualifying.
  • More rain.
  • A double entry fee.

Iron mode, for the brave.

Chosen when you start a career and fixed after that. Every race you start counts: leaving a session without a result is a retirement with last place and no prize money. In return every race pays 10% more prize money and reputation.

  • Damage is fixed at 100% and repairs cost the full amount.
  • A balance below zero must be settled before the next start, by selling a car or taking an advance.
  • If nothing covers the debt, the career ends in the hall of fame.

As much career as you like.

A Basic career is for players who just want to race: licences, cups, prize money, dealers and garage. Teams, sponsors, press, the driver market, the gala, duels, career goals and pressure are off. Pick it when you start a career and switch between Basic and Full in the settings while no cup is running.

  • Short season: cups run about half of their rounds and keep the first round and the final.
  • With a short season, entry fee and bonuses per cup drop in the same ratio. Prize money and points per round stay.
  • There is no Basic career in iron mode.
