The race weekend, rivals with a memory, a regular cast of drivers and much more variety.

Race weekend & AIDone · ships with 0.2.0

Qualifying that counts

Qualify before the race: your grid slot, best lap and gap to pole are scored, pole position pays a bonus and goes into your driver file. Choose qualifying alone or with a free practice session first, which you can end at any time.

Being checked in the game: the full weekend from practice to the race.

Race weekend & AIDone · ships with 0.2.0

A field that follows your pace

After every race your best lap is compared with the fastest opponents, and within the AI level you choose the field gets up to three points faster or slower. Shown openly before and after each race and can be switched off. Prize money stays with the AI level, and how the AI drives stays as the game makes it.

Being checked in the game: how strongly the field reacts over a whole cup.

Community wish
Race weekend & AIDone · ships with 0.2.0

Rivals with a memory

Your closest rival gets a card before each race with grid slot, head-to-head record and current form. Rivals move up the licence ladder with you, every opponent has a form curve over the season, and your driver file tells the story of each rivalry: first duel, closest finish, latest meeting.

Being checked in the game: how much a driver's form shows on track.

Race weekend & AIDone · ships with 0.2.0

A real season finale

Match point: before any race that can decide the title you see what is enough, even before the finale. Win the title early and it gets its own moment in the round summary and your driver file. Come back to a cup you won to defend your crown.

Race weekend & AIDone · ships with 0.2.0

A regular cast of drivers

112 hand-made fictional drivers, the same people in every career: young talents who get faster as you progress, veterans who slowly fade, gentleman drivers, team pairings and rivalries in the paddock. Each has a home country and strengths or weaknesses in the rain, on fast or tight tracks and in their form, shown on the driver card. Now and then one of eight legends turns up as a guest, with a story that may sound familiar.

Being checked in the game: how much rain, track type and home races change a driver's pace.

VarietyDone · ships with 0.2.0

Race formats

About every third race comes with a twist, fixed when you enter and announced in the cup calendar: a reversed top six without qualifying, success ballast for the three leading opponents, an extra point for the fastest lap or a rain race whatever the forecast says. Can be switched off in the settings.

Being checked in the game: success ballast for your own car and performance balancing.

VarietyDone · ships with 0.2.0

Three times as many sponsors

36 fictional brands from tyres, oil, watches, logistics, tech, fashion and more. Small local brands back you at national level, big names only further up, and each has favourite goals and its own way of paying. With qualifying switched on, some want a top qualifying spot or a pole position.

VarietyDone · ships with 0.2.0

More invitations

20 guest events instead of 8, now at every licence level: collector races, club meets with the members' cars, one-make races where everyone drives the same model, and a duel against your closest rival with a small field. Beat your rival and it shows in your reputation.

Being checked in the game: duels with a very small field.

VarietyDone · ships with 0.2.0

Teams in the paddock

12 fictional teams race in the paddock with two drivers each, and every cup has team standings. With a team seat or a works contract you take one of the two seats and race for the team title alongside your team-mate.

PresentationDone · ships with 0.2.0

Trophies and title scenes

A trophy for every licence level in gold, silver or bronze for the top three of a cup. It stands next to your title car in the title scene, at the final standings and in a trophy cabinet in your driver file.

Being checked in the game: how the trophies look in the game's own renderer.

PresentationDone · ships with 0.2.0

Smooth transitions

Pages, results, medals and trophies move in with calm transitions, and the finishing order runs in line by line. In VR everything only fades, nothing moves.

Economy & teamDone · ships with 0.2.0

Season sponsor

A main sponsor for a whole licence level: a signing fee, a bonus for every race that meets its goal, for every cup in the top three and for your first title. Miss the top three twice and the sponsor walks away. Each offer has its own style: solid, ambitious or clean. Cup sponsors stay.

Economy & teamDone · ships with 0.2.0

Team-mates

With a team seat or a works contract you get a named AI team-mate in the same car. The team keeps score of your duel over the cup and wants you ahead at the end.
