The same people in every career.

112 hand-made fictional drivers in 30 teams. Young talents get faster as you progress, veterans slowly fade, and gentleman drivers fill the grid. Each has a home country, good and bad days and strengths or weaknesses, shown on the driver card.

Rivals with a memory.

Your closest rival gets a card before each race with grid slot, head-to-head record and current form. Drivers you had close fights with remember it, move up the licence ladder with you and turn up again in later cups.

  • 17 rivalries already run through the paddock.
  • Your driver file tells the story of each rivalry, from the first duel to the latest meeting.

Teams and team-mates.

Every team runs two drivers, and every cup has team standings. With a team seat or a works contract you take one of the two seats and race for the team title alongside a named team-mate in the same car.
